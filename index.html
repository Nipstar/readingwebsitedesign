Fair questions

Asked by Reading firms weighing up an agency

Why a web designer and not an agency?

One person, one price and no hand-offs. The honest catch is that one person has limits: there are no in-house copywriters, only Andy working from what you tell him, and he can only run so many builds at once. If you need a large team, an agency is the better call.

Do you cover Wokingham, Earley and Woodley?

Yes, plus Caversham, Tilehurst, Theale and Twyford.

What does SEO cost in Reading?

Local SEO support is £497 a month, covering map-rank reports, review requests and Google profile upkeep. The full scope, and what is left out, is on the Reading SEO page.

What will the website itself cost?

Starter is £600 for five pages and Core is £995 for eight. Further pages are £125, and hosting and care comes to £10 a month. Those figures are the same before and after your first conversation with Andy.

How long will Andy need?

With one person on the job and no queue of approvals, the usual span is 1 to 3 weeks from your deposit.

Would I depend on Andy forever?

No. You own the site once it is paid for. A one-person business should not be a single point of failure for your website, and owning it means you could take it elsewhere.
